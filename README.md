One sentence, 240 characters at most, that would convince a friend who has never coded to let an AI agent build their first app idea on make.fun.

## The pitch

> Just describe the app you've always wanted in plain words, and an AI agent on make.fun will build the first working version for you while you finish your coffee — free to try, and the idea stays yours.

## How it works

`main.ts` (Node 22) writes the pitch to `/output/entry.txt` — nothing else. The entry is the whole submission: no code, no explanation.
